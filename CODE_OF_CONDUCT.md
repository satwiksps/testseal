# Code of Conduct

TestSeal welcomes careful technical disagreement. The project benefits when
people can challenge a detector, question a design choice, or report a false
positive without being dismissed or attacked.

## Expected conduct

- Discuss the code, evidence, and tradeoffs rather than a person's motives or
  competence.
- Be specific. Reproductions, counterexamples, and links to relevant code are
  more useful than labels or speculation.
- Respect privacy. Do not post credentials, private source, personal data, or
  vulnerability details in public issues.
- Give contributors time to respond, especially across time zones and outside
  paid working hours.
- Accept a maintainer decision once the reasoning and available evidence have
  been considered. It is fine to document a respectful disagreement.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, doxxing,
and sustained disruption are not acceptable in project spaces or in private
communication connected to the project.

## Enforcement

For ordinary moderation concerns, contact the maintainer privately through the
contact method on the maintainer's GitHub profile. For security matters, follow
`SECURITY.md` instead. Include links or screenshots when possible and avoid
repeating harmful content more than necessary.

The maintainer may edit or remove content, close or lock discussions, reject
contributions, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Decisions
will consider the severity, context, prior behavior, and the safety of affected
people. Reports will be handled as privately as practical; confidentiality
cannot be promised where disclosure is required for safety or law.

This policy applies to repository discussions, pull requests, issues,
community forums, and public interactions where someone is representing
TestSeal.
